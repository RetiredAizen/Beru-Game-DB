How Dungeon Gates WorkQuick overview of normal gates and Red Gates⌄

STANDARD GATES

Using /dungeon can open an E, D, C, B, A, or S Gate. Standard gates give their reward instantly. Appearance rates, Essence ranges, and artifact drop rates will be added when confirmed.

RED GATES

A Red Gate starts a combat encounter instead of granting only an instant reward. It can roll any internal difficulty from E to S and guarantees an artifact drop when completed.

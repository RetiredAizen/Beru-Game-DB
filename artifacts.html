OBTAINING ARTIFACTS

Artifacts can drop randomly from Gates through the Dungeon command and are also rewarded from Raids. On Hard difficulty, the Raid MVP is guaranteed an artifact drop.

WHY ARTIFACTS MATTER

Artifacts provide a large portion of your Attack, HP, or Defense scaling, depending on the set you equip. They also add Power, which increases your overall damage scaling.

UPGRADE RECOMMENDATION

Essence is valuable, so it is recommended to save it for Legendary artifacts. Lower-rarity sets can be used temporarily, but Legendary sets generally do not take long to farm.

CHOOSING A SET

Hot Summer is currently the most-used option because of its high Crit Rate while giving only slightly less Attack than Burning Curse. Burning Curse focuses on Attack, Burning Greed is an HP set, and Burning Blessing provides Defense scaling. Guild Bosses can have an Attack, Defense, or HP weakness—matching your artifact scaling to that weakness grants bonus damage.

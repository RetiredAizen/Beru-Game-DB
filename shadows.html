XP STRATEGY

Efficient Shadow Feeding

Feeding a shadow into another shadow with the same name grants double XP. Instead of feeding every duplicate directly into your main shadow, chain the duplicates so the stored XP receives the bonus again at each step.

STEP 01Feed a Level 1 duplicate into another Level 1 copy with the same name.
STEP 02Feed the resulting levelled shadow into the next fresh Level 1 duplicate.
STEP 03Repeat the chain, always moving the accumulated XP into the next copy.

Because the accumulated XP is doubled again during each matching-name feed, approximately four to five copies can provide enough XP to max a shadow.

Knight → Commander Method
Build a Knight to Level 40 through duplicate chaining, then feed it into a Level 1 Commander with the passive you want. A Level 40 Knight brings the Commander to approximately Level 47/50.
Important: The receiving shadow survives and keeps its own passive. The consumed shadow's passive is not transferred. Make sure the final Level 1 receiver has the stat and roll you want before completing the chain.

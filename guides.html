QUICK TAKEAWAYS

What Matters Most

PoD & GUILD BOSSPrioritize Total Power

Power directly scales damage. Shadow Crit buffs can also contribute.

STORYRaw Stats Matter

Badges and Core stat percentages apply directly, while Total Power itself does not.

RAIDPower + Crit

Total Power feeds the attack roll. Shadow Essence and EXP buffs do not improve rewards.

DUNGEONSUtility on Normal Gates

Shadow Essence and EXP buffs apply to normal rewards. Power matters only for Red Gates.

TERMS

Mode & Stat Notes

TP means Total Power

It describes your combined Power value; it is not a separate Core currency.

Indirect through Power

The source does not apply its stat directly in that mode, but its Power contribution still improves the result.

Fixed Crit

The mode calculates Crit using its own rules and does not read the Crit passive equipped on your Shadows.

POWER BREAKDOWN

Total Power Sources

Total Power is built from several progression systems. These figures show the highest currently documented contribution from each source without counting its stat bonuses a second time.

◆

Artifacts

46,480 Power

Four Legendary artifacts at Level 50: 11,620 each. Expensive to finish, but their stat and set bonuses also strengthen your build.

◉

Cores

300,000 Power

Three Level-20 Legendary Cores at 100,000 each. You can equip one Mind, one Body and one Spirit Core.

♟

Shadow Army

417,500 Power

Up to ten Level-50 Commanders at 41,750 each. This is the theoretical Commander-only maximum.

⚔

Armory

480,000 Power

Your eight strongest weapons count. With current SR data, Level 40 plus A10 gives 60,000 per weapon. Future SSR weapons will raise this ceiling.

🏆

Legacy Badges

600,000 Power

Five badges grant flat Power: four provide 100,000 each and Curving My Own Path provides 200,000. Raid Stopper and Rookie Destroyer grant effects instead.

⬆

SJW Level

Data pending

A free and important source earned simply by levelling. Its base value and per-level formula still need confirmation.

KNOWN CURRENT MAXIMUM1,843,980 Power
Artifacts + Cores + ten Commanders + eight Level-40 A10 SR weapons + flat Badge Power. Add SJW-level Power on top. This is a documented subtotal, not the final permanent cap.

Best repeatable Essence value

Legendary Cores dominate early Power efficiency. Their later levels remain strong, but require Condensed Energy at both Limit Breaks.

Best non-Essence early route

Daily Shadow catches are random, but duplicate feeding can accelerate early Power dramatically. See the Efficient Shadow Feeding guide.

Power is not everything

Artifacts are expensive per 1,000 Power, but also increase combat stats and provide set bonuses. Badges and advancements are valuable but time-gated.

Still worth confirming: whether any equipped weapon contributes outside the Armory calculation, and whether titles, future systems or SSR weapons add another separate Power source.

Run Your Dungeons

Start here for reliable Essence and a chance to receive Artifacts. You receive 40 free runs daily, with a 60-second cooldown between runs.

Extra keys allow up to 190 additional turns, but later packages become less efficient. New players should generally buy only the first four options until they reach max SJW level and improve their Essence Shadow buffs, Core buffs and Dungeon odds.

Keep Essence-buff Shadows active and salvage duplicate Artifacts for additional Essence. See Dungeon details and the Artifact database.

Attack the Guild Boss

Use all five daily attacks against the current Guild Boss. Attacks have a three-minute cooldown. A boss cycle lasts three days, with a 12-hour break between cycles.

Bosses can have Attack, Defense or HP weaknesses, so adjust your build accordingly. See Guild Boss details.

WEEKLY CHECKLIST

Weekly Challenges

/pod challenge

Power of Destruction

Submit your score at any point during the weekly challenge. There is no cooldown, so you can wait until your build improves before making your serious attempt.

/workshop

Workshop of Brilliant Light

You receive three tickets each week, and unused tickets can be saved. Clear chance depends on your Total Power and selected difficulty. Two extra tickets can be bought weekly for 20,000 Essence each, but they are expensive. Cores are currently the fastest and most Essence-efficient repeatable Power source. See the Cores page.

Simple priority: never miss free claims, shop items, Hunts or weekly tickets. Complete the 40 free Dungeons when possible, use Guild Boss attacks during its active cycle, and fit Raids around their four-hour cooldown.
